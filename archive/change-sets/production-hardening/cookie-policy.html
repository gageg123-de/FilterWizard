Cookie Policy

What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies and similar browser technologies help websites remember preferences, measure usage, and understand how visitors interact with pages. Filter Wizard uses a small first-party preference record for cookie consent and, when accepted, analytics tools that may use cookies or similar technologies.

How Filter Wizard uses storage

Essential storage remembers your cookie choice in your browser under filterWizardConsent. Analytics storage is used only if you accept analytics cookies. Advertising-related consent remains denied by default.

Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity

If you accept analytics, Filter Wizard uses Google Analytics to measure traffic, engagement, Filter Finder usage, and affiliate-link clicks. Filter Wizard also uses Microsoft Clarity to understand usability through behavior analytics such as heatmaps, scrolling, and session recordings. If you decline analytics, Filter Wizard sends denied analytics-storage signals to these tools.

Affiliate links

Amazon affiliate links may include referral information in the URL. Filter Wizard does not need to set its own Amazon cookie before you click an affiliate link. Amazon's own practices may apply after you visit Amazon.

Changing your choice

You can change your analytics choice using Cookie Settings in the footer. You can also remove cookies and browser storage through your browser settings. Removing browser storage may cause the cookie banner to appear again.
