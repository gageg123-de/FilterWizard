Not Sure of Your Filter Size? Here’s Where to Look.

Most disposable air filters have their size printed on the cardboard frame. Look along the outer edge for three numbers, such as 20 × 25 × 1.

No readable markings? Measure the existing filter’s length, width, and thickness, then compare those measurements with the filter holder and manufacturer guidance. Nominal and actual dimensions can differ.
